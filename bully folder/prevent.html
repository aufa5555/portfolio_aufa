How to Prevent Bullying?

Develop a Clear Anti-Bullying Policy

This policy must be clear, concise, and consistently enforced. It should define what bullying is (including cyberbullying), outline the consequences, and provide a reliable reporting procedure. Every student and parent should receive and acknowledge this policy.

Create Safe Spaces

Ensure students know where they can go if they feel unsafe or overwhelmed, and who they can talk to.

Open Communication

Keep an open line of communication with parents about the school's anti-bullying initiatives.

Workshops for Parents

Offer workshops to help parents recognize signs that their child is being bullied or is bullying others, and how to respond effectively.
