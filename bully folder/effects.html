Effects of Bullying

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

In severe cases, the chronic trauma of bullying can lead to PTSD, with symptoms like flashbacks, hypervigilance, and severe anxiety.

Decreased Academic Performance

The stress and anxiety make it difficult to concentrate, leading to a drop in grades.

Loss of Interest in Learning

School becomes associated with fear and pain, stripping away the joy and curiosity of learning.

Self-Harm and Suicidal Behavior

In an attempt to cope with overwhelming emotional pain, some victims may turn to self-harm like cutting or, in the worst cases, attempt suicide.
