What is Bullying?

Bullying is repeated, aggressive behavior intended to cause harm, which involves a real or perceived power imbalance between the person doing the bullying and the victim.Bullying can be physical, verbal and social and includes actions like hitting, threatening, name-calling, excluding others, or spreading rumors.
