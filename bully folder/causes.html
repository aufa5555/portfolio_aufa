Causes of Bullying

Lack of love

Bullies often lack affection at home, which makes them feel unseen. As a result, they bully others to prove their existence..

Lack of Empathy

Some individuals who bully have difficulty understanding or sharing the feelings of others. This lack of empathy allows them to inflict pain without feeling guilt or remorse.

Desire for Power and Dominance

Many bullies are motivated by a desire to feel powerful, popular, or in control. By putting others down, they elevate their own social status and feel a sense of superiority.

Low Self-Esteem

Contrary to the confident image they may project, many bullies actually suffer from low self-worth. Bullying others can be a misguided way to deflect attention from their own insecurities and feel better about themselves.
