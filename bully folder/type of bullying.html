Type of Bullying

Physical Bullying

The use of physical force to harm or intimidate someone, including actions like hitting, kicking, shoving, and tripping.

Verbal Bullying

Using words to hurt, intimidate, or scare someone — such as name-calling, teasing, threatening, and inappropriate comments.

Social Bullying

Intentionally harming someone's reputation or relationships through exclusion, rumor-spreading, and manipulation.
