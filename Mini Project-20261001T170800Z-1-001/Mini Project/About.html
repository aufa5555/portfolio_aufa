About Pesta Air Antarabangsa

Largest Water Festival in Northern Malaysia

Pesta Air Antarabangsa Kuala Perlis 2025 is an annual event that brings communities together through water-based activities, cultural performances, and traditional competitions.

Organized by the Persatuan Khazanah Kuala Perlis and the Perlis State Government in collaboration with the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture, this festival promises an unforgettable experience.

Join us at the Menteri Besar Incorporated (MBI) site to witness the excitement of this international water festival!
