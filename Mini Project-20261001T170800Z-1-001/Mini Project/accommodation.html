Tips for Malaysian Tourists

🏨

Accommodation

  • Book early, especially for 12-16 September
  • Festival dates are peak season
  • Stay in Kuala Perlis for proximity
  • Stay in Kangar for more options & cheaper rates
⏰

Planning & Timing

  • Arrive early to avoid peak crowding
  • Morning is better for visits
  • Allocate extra time for traffic & parking
  • Bring cash (local ringgit)
🎒

What to Bring

  • Lightweight, breathable clothes
  • Sun protection: hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
  • Rain gear: umbrella or poncho
  • Good walking shoes
  • Portable power bank & water bottle
👑

Cultural Conduct

  • Respect local customs
  • Dress modestly in traditional areas
  • Be mindful at water sport areas
  • Don't venture into restricted zones
  • Follow safety guidelines

Important Booking Notice

🚨 Book your accommodation early! The festival period (12-16 September 2025) is peak season and places fill up quickly.

Recommended to book at least 2-3 months in advance for the best options and prices.
