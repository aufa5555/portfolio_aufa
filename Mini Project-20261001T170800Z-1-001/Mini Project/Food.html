Kuala Perlis Food Guide

Kak Su Laksa
📍 97, Lorong Siakap 1, Jalan Kuala Perlis
Famous for authentic Laksa Kuala Perlis - a must-try local delicacy.
Must Try: Laksa Kuala Perlis, Pulut Udang
Api-Api Ikan Bakar
📍 Jalan Dato' Ali, Kuala Perlis
Fresh seafood grilled to perfection in authentic local style.
Must Try: Grilled fish, Fresh seafood selection
Restoran Nyiru Klasik
📍 Near highway / jetty area, Kuala Perlis
Popular seafood restaurant known for fresh catches and flavorful dishes.
Must Try: Siakap 3 Rasa, Ketam Celup Tepung
Dhiya Ikan Bakar & Seafood
📍 Jalan Kuala Perlis, Pekan Kuala Perlis
Perfect for dinner by the sea with relaxed ambience and fresh seafood.
Must Try: Ikan Bakar, Fresh squid and prawns
Restoran Api-Api
📍 Kuala Perlis
Well-known seafood spot famous for delicious grilled dishes.
Must Try: Ikan Bakar, Butter Prawn
Kuala Perlis Seafood Restaurant
📍 Jalan Kuala Perlis, Kangar (near pier)
Classic Malaysian style seafood restaurant, great for group dining.
Must Try: Spicy Prawns, Fried Squid
Laksa Perlis
Rich fish gravy with distinctive Perlis style, served with rice noodles and fresh herbs.
Pulut Udang
Sticky rice with shrimp paste, usually served as accompaniment with laksa.
Emping / Ringgi
Rice flakes or thin wafers with coconut and sugar - a crispy local snack.
Pekasam Puyu
Preserved puyu fish, salted, dried and fried with onions - unique local delicacy.
Pulut Mempelam
Sticky rice served with Harum Manis mango when in season - sweet tropical treat.
