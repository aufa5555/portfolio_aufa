About the Festival

History & Origins

The Kuala Perlis Water Festival was first organized in 1961 as a traditional state event in Perlis. It was originally shaped by the lifestyle and culture of the local communities living along river estuaries and coastal areas, particularly fishing villages. Traditional boats, water-based activities, and strong community bonds were key elements of the celebration.

Modern Development

Since 2019, the festival faced several interruptions due to the COVID-19 pandemic. It was revived in 2024 with more activities such as decorated boat contests, traditional races, cultural shows, themed nights, and light displays. By 2025, it gained international status with teams from Thailand, Brunei, the Philippines, Indonesia, Australia, and other Western countries.
