Kuala Perlis Travel Guide

Local Transport

By Road (Car / Bus)

From within Malaysia:

  • Drive or take bus to Perlis state
  • Capital: Kangar → Kuala Perlis (coastal)
  • Use North-South Expressway
  • Continue via local roads

By Train + Bus/Taxi

Train Options:

  • KTM to Alor Setar or Padang Besar
  • Alight at nearest station
  • Continue by road to Kuala Perlis
  • Local bus or taxi available

By Flight + Road

Airport Options:

  • Alor Setar Airport (~1 hour)
  • Penang Airport (~2.5 hours)
  • Langkawi Airport (~1.5 hours + ferry)
  • Then road transport to Kuala Perlis

Travel Tips

🎫
Book ferry tickets in advance during peak season
🦐
Try local seafood at waterfront restaurants
🐍
Visit nearby snake and reptile farm
💵
Carry cash - some places don't accept cards
👟
Wear comfortable shoes for exploring
📅
Best time to visit: November to February
🌅
Don't miss the sunset at Kuala Perlis jetty
💰
Bargain at local markets for better prices
